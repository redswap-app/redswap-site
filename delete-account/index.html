Delete your RedSwap account

RedSwap, the shift-trade app for firefighters

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open RedSwap and sign in.
  2. Tap your picture at the top of the Board tab to open your Profile.
  3. Tap Edit profile, then Delete my account, and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away.

Without the app

Email support@redswap.app from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my RedSwap account". We'll confirm and delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, email address, phone number and profile picture.
  • Your department memberships, rank, certifications and schedule.
  • Your open posts and offers, alerts and settings.
  • Your business pages with their photos, locations, services, products and reviews.
  • Your calendar link, reminders and Trusted Reviewer status.

What is kept

  • Completed trades, IOUs and trade ratings are kept, because other members' records depend on them, with your name replaced by "Deleted member".
  • Business reviews you wrote and "I hired them" marks you gave are deleted.
  • Backups are cleared within 30 days.
