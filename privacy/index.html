RedSwap Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

RedSwap is a shift-trade app for firefighters, with a directory of businesses run by firefighters and their families. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. "We" means RedSwap, operated by Matthew Green. Questions: support@redswap.app.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address, username and profile picture.
  • Department membership: the departments you join, your rank, EMS level, certifications and platoon, and whether your crew has verified you.
  • Schedule and trades: your rotation, days you mark as working, off or unavailable, the shifts you post or accept, IOUs, paybacks and trade ratings.
  • Phone number (optional): stored separately and shown only to members you have a trade or "text for info" contact with, or on a business page if you put it there.
  • Union membership (optional): if your union has a page on RedSwap and its board confirms you're a member (or you're on the board), we record that, and a union member badge shows on your profile and business page. You can hide the badge or leave the union page at any time. If your union president gives you a RedSwap Pro seat, we record that too. If you vote in a poll on a union or association page, we record your vote so you can change it; everyone sees only the totals, never who voted for what.
  • Hours log (optional): overtime, sick and vacation time, holidays, comp time, training and exposures (with an incident number if you add one) that you choose to log. Only you can see your log. You can change, delete or export it at any time, and it is deleted with your account.
  • Certifications and licenses (optional): the names, expiry dates and numbers you choose to add, so RedSwap can remind you before they expire. Only you can see them, and they are deleted with your account.
  • Business pages (optional): business name, description, trades, services and products, photos, business card images, phone, website and links, and the ZIP codes of your locations.
  • Reviews and reports: star ratings, written reviews, "I hired them" marks, and reports you send about content.
  • Feedback: bug reports and ideas you send, with the page you were on and your phone type.
  • Phone notifications (optional): if you turn them on, your phone's notification token, linked to your RedSwap account so alerts reach the right phone.
  • Usage: the last time you used the app, and directory searches (used to improve search and spot missing trades).

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, messages, or payment card details. RedSwap has no ads and no ad tracking.

Your calendars (optional): if you turn on "This phone's calendar", or connect a Google or Microsoft (Outlook) account, RedSwap reads your calendar events so it can show them next to your shifts. Events on your phone are read on the phone. Events from Google or Microsoft are read with read-only access, straight from Google's or Microsoft's servers onto your phone. RedSwap never sends them to its own servers, never stores, sells or shares them, and uses them for nothing else. You can turn this off or disconnect an account at any time in the app (Profile, Calendar sync), in your phone's settings, or in your Google or Microsoft account settings. If you turn on "Put my shifts in this phone's calendar", RedSwap writes your shifts, trades, paybacks, meetings and time off (never exposures) into a calendar named RedSwap on your phone; turning it off removes that calendar.

RedSwap's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show posts to members who can cover them, match trades, keep your calendar and IOUs, and run the business directory.
  • To contact you about your account and trades: trade confirmations, reminders and alerts, in the app, by email or as phone notifications. You choose which ones in the app.
  • To keep RedSwap safe: verification, reports, and removing content that breaks the rules.

Who can see what

  • Your shift posts are visible to verified members of the department you posted in.
  • Verified members of a department can see its member list: the name, photo, rank, platoon and EMS level of everyone verified there, and the union member badge of those who show it. Tapping a name opens that member's profile.
  • Your name, department and ratings are visible to members you trade with.
  • Business pages, reviews and photos are visible to verified RedSwap members, as described in the app.
  • Your union member badge, unless you hide it, is visible to members who can see your profile or business page. The board of your union's page sees who asked to be confirmed, and the union president sees who holds the union's Pro seats. People who can read your union's page can also find you in its member list, unless you hide the badge; the board sees every confirmed member.
  • A state or regional association's page on RedSwap is visible to verified members of the departments it covers. Its board sees how many verified members each covered department has, and can look up verified members of those departments by name to add them to the association's board, the same way a local's president can.
  • Reports are reviewed by the RedSwap team and, for some reports, by Trusted Reviewers, who never see who sent a report.

Service providers

We don't sell your data or share it for advertising. We use these providers only to run RedSwap: Supabase (database, sign-in and photo storage), Resend (email) and OneSignal (phone notifications: it receives your phone's notification token, your RedSwap account ID and the text of each alert, and delivers it through Apple or Google). If you add RedSwap's calendar link to Google, Apple or Outlook Calendar, that calendar service fetches your shifts from us. If you connect a Google or Microsoft account to show your own calendar in RedSwap, your phone signs in to that company and reads your events directly from it; RedSwap's servers aren't involved.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is open. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile, Edit profile, Delete my account) or by following the steps at redswap.app/delete-account. Deleting your account removes your profile, phone number, schedule, open posts, business pages, photos and the reviews you wrote. Records another member also relies on, such as a completed trade, an IOU or a trade rating, are kept with your name replaced by "Deleted member". Backups are cleared within 30 days.

Security

All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access is limited by account so members only see what the app is designed to show them.

Children

RedSwap is for adults (18 and over) who work for fire departments. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters.
